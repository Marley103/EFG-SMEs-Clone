FAQs

What is SME financing from EFG Finance SMEs ?

+

This financing product supports established small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs), with financing amounts ranging from EGP 250,000 to EGP 10 million.

Is my business eligible for financing from EFG Finance SMEs?

+

To qualify, the business need to have been operational for a minimum of 2 years and need to provide financial statements for the last financial year, prepared by a certified accountant.

What is the loan duration for SME financing from EFG Finance SMEs?

+

The loan duration ranges from 3 to 12 months

How long does it take to approve and disburse the loan?

+

Approval typically takes 1-3 working days, and disbursement occurs 1 working day after contract signing.
